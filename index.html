The video opens with a group of friends enjoying food and drinks around a picnic table in a wooded area. Everything seems perfectly ordinary until a large American black bear makes its way over and begins helping itself to whatever looks appealing. Instead of immediately moving away, the friends remain seated, laughing and taking pictures as the bear explores the table. The animal seems particularly interested in the food, although it doesn’t appear especially concerned about the humans sitting just inches away.

Then comes the part that makes this encounter so unusual. One of the men pulls out a loaf of bread and starts preparing a sandwich. When the bear leans closer to investigate, he raises his hand as though telling it to wait its turn. He then finishes making a peanut butter sandwich and feeds it directly to the animal. It’s an almost unbelievable interaction between people and a powerful wild animal. And while everyone appears to get through the encounter without incident, there are some important reasons why experts would advise against repeating it.
